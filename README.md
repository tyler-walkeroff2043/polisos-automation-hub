## What PolisOS Does

PolisOS is designed for decentralized governance in DAO environments and web3 communities. It combines proposal drafting, participant role management, and vote coordination into one web interface, reducing the overhead of moving between tools during a governance cycle.

Teams that want a more organized consensus process and a reliable record of decisions can use PolisOS to keep work visible and structured. The platform includes an audit trail, a transparent decision ledger, multilingual support, and AI-assisted governance capabilities to help coordinate collective action without making the workflow harder to manage.

## How to Use

The PolisOS workflow is intentionally straightforward:

1. Draft or create a proposal.
2. Assign roles to the people involved in review or execution.
3. Coordinate discussion and consensus.
4. Execute the voting process.
5. Inspect the audit trail and decision ledger once the result is recorded.

For distributed teams, you can move between supported languages as needed and rely on the web interface to keep governance activity in one organized place.
